**Structure:** AIDA, Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
**Core insight:** traders don't buy learning, they buy winning, consistency, profit, passing and keeping funded accounts, and the confidence of being in control. Sell the win. Deliver it through learning.
**Voice:** calm, honest, trader-to-trader. Natural, not salesy: no hype, no fear hooks, no promises Proof can't keep (passing a challenge, "fixing" anything). No invented numbers or testimonials, example insights are clearly marked as examples until real ones exist. Only claim what is built today.
**Desire:** write from what traders actually want (consistency, trust in their own edge) without naming it. Show their situation and the outcome, never the feeling ("you open your week knowing which setup to trust", not "feel in control"). No feature lists.

## INTEREST, Why journaling hasn't worked

**Headline:** Every trader knows their journal could make them better. Almost none keep one.

Not because you lack discipline. Because the moment a trade closes, when the truth about why you took it is still fresh, the journal asks you to open a form and type numbers your platform already has.

So the moment passes. And the mistakes that drain your account stay invisible, repeating quietly week after week.

**Proof removes the work and keeps the truth.**

---

## INTEREST, How it works

**1. Connect once.**
Link your MetaTrader account once. Your trades, past and future, come in on their own, so there's no typing entries, stops or results.

**2. Say what happened.**
When a trade closes, Proof asks one question: *what happened?* Type it or speak it, the way you'd tell a friend. Keep it as written, or let Proof organise it around your setups. A few seconds, and it's done.

**3. See where the money goes.**
Ask your journal anything, like "Which setup makes me the most?" or "How do I trade after a loss?" You get answers from your own trades, with every trade behind the answer one tap away.

## DESIRE, What changes

**Headline:** Trade with the clarity of someone who knows their numbers.

**Journaling that fits between trades.**
No forms. No typing numbers. No setting aside an hour on Sunday. A few seconds after a trade, from your phone or your desk, and it's done. When journaling is this easy, you keep doing it. That's where every other benefit starts.

**Find your edge.**
See which setups, pairs and sessions make you money, so you can do more of what works.

**Spot the leaks.**
See the habits quietly costing you: trading after losses, oversizing when confident, entries you take out of boredom. Once you can see them, you can work on them.

**Trade your plan under pressure.**
For prop traders, consistency matters most. Proof shows whether you're actually following your plan, so a bad week doesn't quietly become a habit.

**Know where you stand.**
You open your week already knowing which setup to trust, which to leave alone, and the one small thing worth trying next.

*Questions you can ask your journal.* Each opens to Ask's real answer on the sample journal, labelled "from a sample journal" with its confidence. Never results presented as the trader's own:
- "How do my trades do in the hour after a loss?" → "Trades you opened within the hour after a loss (2 trades) won 50%, with +$90 a trade. The rest (33 trades) won 33%, with +$45.82." *Not enough data · 2 trades*
- "Which setup makes me the most?" → "3-touch makes you the most money: +$1,404 over 17 trades (41% win rate). Next is Footprint at +$396." *Possible pattern · 17 trades*
- "Do I do better when I follow my plan?" → "When you followed your plan (11 trades), you won 55% with +$129 a trade. When you didn't (14 trades), it was 0% and −$82.29." *Possible pattern · 11 trades*

## DESIRE, Built around how you trade

**Headline:** Your system. Not ours.

Describe your playbook in plain words, your setups, your rules, what you care about. Proof organises everything around it. It never tells you how to trade. It shows you how *you* trade.

## DESIRE, The trades you didn't take matter too

Log what forms never capture:
- **Missed trades:** "Saw it, didn't take it, would've been +$400."
- **Broken rules:** "Revenge trade after the loss."
- **Plans:** "Watching gold at 2,340 for a footprint entry."
- **Your state:** "Slept badly. Cut size."

Your discipline is part of your edge. Proof makes it visible.

---

## DESIRE, Answers you can trust

Proof never makes up a number. Every figure is calculated from your actual trades, and every answer shows the trades it came from. If your data can't answer a question, Proof tells you. And when something looks worth changing, it suggests a small experiment to try, not an order.

## DESIRE, Private by default

- Proof can see your trades. It can never place or change one.
- Your login details are locked away. Disconnect any time and they're deleted.
- No voice recordings stored.
- Export or delete your data whenever you want.

## FAQ

**Do I have to talk to it?**
No. Type or speak, whatever suits the moment.

**Does it work with prop-firm accounts?**
Yes, if your firm uses MetaTrader 4 or 5. Connect with your investor password, the view-only one your firm gives you.

**Will it tell me how to trade?**
No. Your strategy stays yours. Proof shows you, with evidence, what's working in it and what isn't.

**Where is my journal stored?**
In your Proof account, so it's there on every phone and computer you sign in on. Only you can see it, and you can export or delete it any time.

**What does it cost?**
Free during early access.
